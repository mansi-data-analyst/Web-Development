The Best Movies According to Mansi

My Top 3 of all time favorites

3 Idiots

A comedy-drama film about three engineering students and their journey through life.

Conjuring

A supernatural horror film based on real-life paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren.

Insidious

A horror film that follows a family as they try to prevent evil spirits from trapping their comatose child in a realm called The Further.
